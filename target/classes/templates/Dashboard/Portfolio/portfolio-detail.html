Project Overview

Sherni was an independent e-commerce concept created to explore the development of an online women's fashion store.

Built around WordPress and WooCommerce, the project focused on establishing a distinct brand identity and designing a fashion-oriented storefront.

The initial concept included a promotional homepage, product category sections, featured collections, customer navigation and social media integration. The project remained at the initial concept and design stage.

Project Features

This section describes the main features and functionality explored during the project.

  • Homepage and promotional banner design
  • Product category layouts
  • Featured product collections
  • Individual product presentation
  • Shopping cart interface concept
  • Customer login and registration layouts
